gc-app

A private Google Workspace integration for a self-hosted personal assistant

gc-app connects a small, fixed set of Google accounts to a self-hosted AI agent (Hermes) that runs on a private server operated by Erik Chang. It lets the account owner ask the assistant, through a private chat channel, to read their calendar, create or update events, triage and draft email, and look up files, contacts, documents, and spreadsheets.

The app is not offered to the public. Only the operator and the Google accounts he administers (for example, lab accounts of the Attention and Cognition Lab, National Central University) authorize it. There is no sign-up, no advertising, and no sale of data.

What the app does with Google

ServiceUse
Google CalendarList upcoming events; create, update, or delete events when the account owner asks.
GmailSearch and read messages, apply labels, and send or reply to email when the account owner asks.
Google Drive, DocsSearch and read files (read-only).
Google SheetsRead and update spreadsheets the account owner points to.
Google ContactsLook up contact details (read-only).
