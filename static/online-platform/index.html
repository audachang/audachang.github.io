反應時間與刺激呈現時間的穩定度與速度

PsychoJS／Pavlovia、jsPsych、Gorilla、lab.js、Testable 等平台的第三方量測證據，以及對研究設計的含意。

01 · 問題拆解

線上計時其實是三個互相獨立的問題

刺激呈現

時長只能是 frame 的整數倍：60 Hz = 16.7 ms，120 Hz = 8.3 ms，144 Hz = 6.9 ms。瀏覽器可能掉 frame，使時長偏長或偏短。

反應時間

USB 輪詢（一般鍵盤約 8 ms）、作業系統、瀏覽器事件佇列與時間戳疊加。多為固定偏移，少部分是隨機雜訊。

音訊

Web Audio 輸出延遲因裝置而異，網頁端只能估計，無法精確得知。

固定偏移在受試者內設計中會相消；隨機雜訊可用少量額外試次補償；掉 frame 與音訊起始漂移才會直接破壞需要精確時長的作業。

02 · 視覺刺激呈現

多數平台在桌機 Chrome 上，試次間變異只有數毫秒

視覺時長的試次間變異範圍（ms），Bridges et al. (2020) Table 3，跨瀏覽器與作業系統

024681012 ms
  • Anwyl-Irvine et al. (2021)：lab.js 視覺延遲最大 16.49 ms，即 60 Hz 下一個 frame。
  • Pronk et al. (2020)：rAF 使 81.0% 的試次剛好是要求的時長；CSS animation 僅約 24%。macOS 的 Chrome 與 Safari 在 100 ms 以內的短刺激常偏短。

03 · 反應時間

精密度尚可，絕對延遲才是問題

< 10 ms

試次間 SD。Bridges et al.：所有情況皆在 10 ms 內，PsychoJS 在所有組合都低於 4 ms。Gorilla 的 SD 為 8.25 ms（Anwyl-Irvine et al.）。

25–100 ms

絕對延遲：比真實反應晚。Gorilla 約 80 ms；Pronk et al. 的觸控與鍵盤裝置約 57–96 ms；iOS 最少，macOS 最多。

0.12–0.21

裝置雜訊使絕對 RT 信度的下降幅度；相對 RT（條件差）僅下降 0.012 以下（Pronk et al.）。

結論：受試者內、條件相減的分析可靠。絕對 RT 與跨受試者比較，約需多 10% 受試者（Pronk；Reimers & Stewart, 2015）。

04 · 音訊

瀏覽器平台最弱的一環

  • Bridges et al.：「沒有任何線上系統能提供起始時間精確的音訊」，視聽同步所有套件都在苦戰。
  • Gorilla 的音訊延遲依瀏覽器約 88 ms 至 200 ms 以上（取自第二手整理，待核對原表）。
  • Monen et al. (2025)，Electron 桌面版：音訊播放約晚 26–30 ms，錄音起始偏差 59–85 ms。

需要精確聲音起始或視聽同步的作業，不建議放在線上，或另做外部量測。

07 · 伺服器端穩定度

公開資訊遠少於計時精確度

  • Pavlovia：2025-12-27 至 28 日因安全更新停機約一天；token 每 2 小時過期；PsychoPy 2025.1.0 之前的版本無法登入。已上線研究的受試者端不受影響（停機時段除外）。
  • Gorilla、Testable、Labvanced、FindingFive：未找到 uptime 紀錄或 SLA。
  • JATOS（Apache 2.0）：可自架，穩定度取決於機構；ESCoP 的 MindProbe 提供免費雲端實例。
  • 招募：Prolific 學術服務費 33.3%。有報導稱 MTurk 自 2026-07-30 起不再接新客戶（二手來源，未見 Amazon 公告）。

穩定度若是關鍵條件，請直接向供應商索取正式 uptime，或先做小規模壓力測試。

08 · 設計與驗證建議

把計時問題轉成可控的設計條件

分析

  • 受試者內設計，用條件差
  • 避免跨人比較絕對 RT
  • 鍵盤雜訊約損失 0.05 的檢定力，可多 5 人或每條件多 5–10 試次（Gómez et al. 2025 預印本）

實作

  • 以 frame 數指定時長，用 rAF
  • 預先載入所有素材
  • 記錄更新率、瀏覽器、OS、裝置
  • 避免約 50 ms 以下的精確短刺激（如 masked priming）

驗證

  • 對自己的刺激與典型硬體做光電二極體或機械手指量測
  • 音訊另行驗證
  • 在論文中報告驗證結果（Plant & Quinlan, 2013）

最缺的是 2020 年之後的重測

Online experiment platforms:
stability and speed of response-time and stimulus-timing

Third-party measurements of PsychoJS/Pavlovia, jsPsych, Gorilla, lab.js, Testable and others, and what they mean for study design.

Erik Chang · Institute of Cognitive Neuroscience, National Central University
Use ← → keys or the buttons at the bottom to navigate

Compiled from the literature and official documentation; no hardware measurements were made by the author. Web retrieval was at summary level, so please check numbers against the original papers before citing.

01 · BREAKING DOWN THE PROBLEM

Online timing is really three separate problems

Stimulus presentation

Durations can only be whole multiples of a frame: 60 Hz = 16.7 ms, 120 Hz = 8.3 ms, 144 Hz = 6.9 ms. Browsers can drop frames, making a stimulus too long or too short.

Response time

USB polling (about 8 ms for a typical keyboard), the operating system, the browser event queue and timestamp resolution add up. Mostly a fixed offset, with a smaller random component.

Audio

Web Audio output latency varies by device, and a web page can only estimate it, not know it exactly.

A fixed offset cancels out in within-subject designs, and random noise can be offset with a few extra trials. Dropped frames and drifting audio onsets are what directly break tasks that need exact durations.

02 · VISUAL STIMULUS PRESENTATION

On desktop Chrome, most platforms show only a few ms of trial-to-trial variability

Range of trial-to-trial variability of visual duration (ms), Bridges et al. (2020) Table 3, across browsers and operating systems

024681012 ms
  • Anwyl-Irvine et al. (2021): lab.js had a maximum visual delay of 16.49 ms, one frame at 60 Hz.
  • Pronk et al. (2020): rAF produced exactly the requested duration on 81.0% of trials versus about 24% for CSS animation. On macOS, Chrome and Safari often shortened stimuli under 100 ms.

03 · RESPONSE TIME

Precision is acceptable; absolute latency is the problem

< 10 ms

Trial-to-trial SD. Bridges et al.: under 10 ms in all cases, and under 4 ms for PsychoJS in every combination. Gorilla's SD was 8.25 ms (Anwyl-Irvine et al.).

25–100 ms

Absolute latency: recorded RTs are later than true responses. Gorilla about 80 ms; touchscreen and keyboard devices in Pronk et al. about 57–96 ms, lowest on iOS and highest on macOS.

0.12–0.21

Drop in absolute-RT reliability caused by device noise; relative RT (condition difference) dropped by 0.012 or less (Pronk et al.).

Bottom line: within-subject, condition-difference analyses are reliable. Absolute RTs and between-subject comparisons need roughly 10% more participants (Pronk; Reimers & Stewart, 2015).

04 · AUDIO

The weakest link on browser platforms

  • Bridges et al.: "no online system can yet provide audio stimuli with precisely timed onsets," and audio-visual synchrony is a struggle for every package.
  • Gorilla audio latency was roughly 88 ms to over 200 ms depending on browser (from a secondary summary; check against the original table).
  • Monen et al. (2025), Electron desktop app: audio playback about 26–30 ms late, recording onset off by 59–85 ms.

Tasks that need precise sound onset or audio-visual synchrony are not recommended online, or should be validated with external measurement.

07 · SERVER-SIDE STABILITY

Public information is much thinner than for timing precision

  • Pavlovia: about one day of downtime on 27–28 Dec 2025 for a security update; tokens expire every 2 hours; PsychoPy versions before 2025.1.0 could not log in. Participants in already-running studies were not affected, outside the downtime window.
  • Gorilla, Testable, Labvanced, FindingFive: no uptime record or SLA found.
  • JATOS (Apache 2.0): self-hostable, so stability depends on your institution; ESCoP's MindProbe offers a free cloud instance.
  • Recruitment: Prolific charges a 33.3% academic service fee. Reports say MTurk stopped accepting new customers on 2026-07-30 (secondary sources; no Amazon announcement seen).

If stability is a hard requirement, ask the vendor for formal uptime figures or run a small stress test first.

08 · DESIGN AND VALIDATION RECOMMENDATIONS

Turn timing error into a controllable design condition

Analysis

  • Within-subject designs, condition differences
  • Avoid comparing absolute RT across people
  • Keyboard noise costs about 0.05 power; add 5 participants or 5–10 trials per condition (Gómez et al. 2025 preprint)

Implementation

  • Specify durations in frames and use rAF
  • Preload all assets
  • Log refresh rate, browser, OS, device
  • Avoid precise stimuli under about 50 ms (e.g., masked priming)

Validation

  • Measure your own stimuli on typical hardware with a photodiode or robotic finger
  • Validate audio separately
  • Report validation in the paper (Plant & Quinlan, 2013)

09 · GAPS AND ITEMS TO CHECK

What is missing most is post-2020 re-testing

  • Gaps: no systematic external measurements found for jsPsych 8, the current Pavlovia, 120/144 Hz displays, recent Safari and Firefox, Android, web VRR, or gamepad latency.
  • Conflicting numbers: some individual Bridges et al. figures (e.g., Gorilla Safari 30.11 ms) disagree with the Table 3 ranges; the upper bound of Pronk et al.'s RT overestimate was read once as about 96 ms and once as about 115 ms. Check the original tables before citing.
  • Next step: with a photodiode and Arduino, measure 16.7, 50 and 100 ms visual durations and key-press RT for jsPsych 8, PsychoJS/Pavlovia and Gorilla on Windows Chrome, macOS Safari and macOS Chrome.
